Below is my simple recipe.  If you liked it sweeter, spicier, and or more sour, you can add more than what is listed.  Feel free to experiment with my recipe.

1.  3 peppers
2.  4 garlic cloves 
3.  2 teaspoons of sugar
4.  8 small baby tomatoes
5.  1 teaspoon of fish sauce
6.  1 teaspoon of lime
7.  2 teaspoons of Ka's Papaya Sauce
8.  2 handful of shredded papaya
